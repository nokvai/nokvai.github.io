Privacy Policy for Metal Profiler

Last updated: September 24, 2026

NokNokware ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Metal Profiler mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect:

Account information

  • Email address associated with your sign-in
  • Display name / profile name
  • Password when you use email/password sign-in
  • Google account identifiers when you use Google Sign-In

Profile and community content you provide

  • Metal Résumé profile details (username, bio, genres, influences, instruments, location labels you choose to share)
  • Photos you upload (profile avatar, cover image, composition artwork, verification evidence)
  • Favorite songs and compositions (metadata and external links only — we do not store audio or video files)
  • Gear / rig items you list on your profile
  • Playlists and groups you create or join
  • Scene posts you create (text and optional media links or embeds)
  • Direct messages you send to other users
  • Follow relationships and in-app notifications related to your account
  • Verification requests and reviewer notes for Legit Metalhead authenticity checks
  • Safety records: reports you file about posts, comments, messages, or profiles (reason, optional details, and a snapshot of the reported content), users you block, and the date you accepted the Terms of Use

Device and technical information

  • Basic technical data needed to run the App (such as device type and app version)
  • Advertising identifier (Advertising ID / IDFA) when ads are enabled, used by Google AdMob to serve and measure ads

We do not sell your personal information.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information above to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Operate profiles, music links, gear, playlists, groups, scene posts, search, following, messaging, and verification
  • Deliver and display ads through Google AdMob
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, and improve the App
  • Review reports, remove content that violates our Terms of Use, restrict abusive accounts, and enforce blocks between users
  • Respond to support requests and meet legal obligations

3. How Information Is Shared

Other users may see content you choose to make public, such as:

  • Public Metal Résumé profiles, photos, genres, and music links
  • Scene posts you publish
  • Follow counts and public follow relationships
  • Legit Metalhead verification status when verified

Direct messages are visible only to participants in that conversation (and to authorized reviewers if needed for abuse or safety investigations).

When you report content, authorized moderators see the report, the reported content, and your username. The person you report is not told who reported them. Blocks are private: the blocked person is not notified.

Email addresses are never shown on public profiles.

We share data with service providers that help us operate the App, including:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, file storage, realtime messaging delivery, and related backend services
  • Google — Google Sign-In and Google AdMob (advertising)

AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data related to ads according to Google’s policies. On iOS, you may be asked for tracking permission; declining does not block core app features.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a business transfer.

Google Sign-In and AdMob are governed in part by Google’s own policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy
AdMob help: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

4. Camera and Photos

The App may request camera or photo-picker access so you can capture or select profile photos, artwork, and verification evidence. Photos you upload may be stored on our backend and shown according to the features you use. Gallery access uses the system photo picker where available; we do not request broad device media-library permission.

Verification evidence is stored privately and is visible only to you and authorized reviewers.

5. Children’s Privacy

Metal Profiler is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it. See also our Child Safety Standards.

6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain information as long as needed to operate the App, maintain security, and meet legal obligations.

You may delete your account in the App:

  1. Sign in to Metal Profiler
  2. Open Profile
  3. Open the menu and tap Delete account, then confirm

Account deletion removes your Metal Profiler profile, all photos and files you uploaded (avatar, cover, artwork, post, comment, and message images, and verification evidence), follows, friendships, blocks, music links associated with your profile, gear listings, playlists, groups you own where feasible, scene posts, direct messages associated with your account where feasible, verification requests, and related notifications. This cannot be undone.

Reports you filed about other users are kept without your account link so moderators can finish reviewing them. We may retain limited records required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons for as long as those obligations last, then delete or anonymize them.

If you cannot open the App, email benjaminthethird@gmail.com and request account deletion (see Delete your account). Include the email address used to sign in.

7. Your Choices

You can:

  • Edit or remove profile, music, and post information in the App
  • Make your profile private
  • Report content or users, and block or unblock users (Profile menu > Blocked users)
  • Decline camera / photo permissions in device settings (some features will not work without them)
  • Delete your account as described above
  • Contact us with privacy questions

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, including Row Level Security on our backend. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. International Processing

Our service providers may process data in countries other than where you live. By using the App, you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in those locations as needed to provide the service.
