Delete your Metal Profiler account

You can delete your Metal Profiler account and associated community data at any time.

How to delete your account (in the app)

  1. Open Metal Profiler and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Open the overflow menu and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. This permanently removes your profile, follows, uploads, posts, messages, and verification data tied to the account.

If you cannot open the app

Email benjaminthethird@gmail.com and request account deletion. Include the email address used to sign in.

What we delete and what we retain

Account deletion removes your Metal Profiler profile, profile images, composition artwork you uploaded, follows, favorite songs / compositions tied to your profile, gear listings, playlists, groups you own where feasible, scene posts, direct messages associated with your account where feasible, verification requests, and related notifications.

We may retain limited records required for legal or security reasons for as long as those obligations last, then delete or anonymize them.

This cannot be undone.
