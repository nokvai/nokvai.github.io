Privacy Policy for SecureGuard Pro

Last reviewed: October 21, 2026 · Publisher: NokNokware

Downloads · Account and data deletion requests

1. Publisher and agency responsibilities

NokNokware publishes SecureGuard Pro for Windows desktop and Android (Android package: com.secureguard.secure_guard_pro). This policy describes the software’s handling of information and support information sent to the publisher. Publisher privacy contact: benjaminthethird@gmail.com.

The agency or organization using the app decides what records to enter, why they are needed, who may access them and how long to keep them. Its own privacy notice, responsible contact and any client instructions must cover those decisions. This publisher policy does not replace that notice or establish a lawful basis for employment monitoring.

2. Records stored on your device

Depending on what authorized users enter, local records include personnel names and contact details, employment and license information, photos, HR qualification and medical or disciplinary notes, client contacts, deployments, attendance, operational and visitor logs, incidents and evidence, equipment custody, duty check-ins, payroll and billing amounts, memoranda, agency details and report signatories.

Local accounts contain usernames, display names, roles and salted password hashes, rather than stored plaintext passwords. Local audit entries record app activity. Free text and attachments can contain additional information about employees, visitors, witnesses or other people. Enter only information needed for an approved purpose; avoid unnecessary identity documents, health details or identifying filenames.

3. Offline operation and external destinations

Online activation and verification send the product, license identifier, installation public key and Windows/Android platform to NokNokware Licensing, hosted by Appwrite Cloud in Singapore. The service stores the license status, installation binding, activation and expiry times, short-lived challenges and hashed rate-limit counters. Activation keys are sent over HTTPS and are not retained in plaintext in the licensing database. Agency records and attachments are not sent for licensing. The device retains an installation credential and signed certificate in operating-system protected storage, separately from agency backups. Appwrite may process network addresses and operational/security logs under its hosting terms and retention settings.

This information is used to issue and verify six-month, single-installation entitlements and prevent unauthorized reuse. Activation and renewal require internet access; a validated installation can use its signed certificate offline until expiry. Requests about publisher-held licensing metadata may be sent to benjaminthethird@gmail.com; deletion can affect license availability and is subject to proportionate verification and applicable retention obligations. It does not erase local agency records. Do not send activation keys or private installation credentials in support requests.

Records are held in SQLite and local attachment files. Each device has independent records. The current app has no publisher-operated record server, automatic record synchronization, advertising, analytics or automatic crash-report upload. Normal record entry does not send those records to NokNokware.

Users can import files, export equipment CSV, save PDF reports, print and create encrypted complete backups. PDFs and CSV files are readable exports and are not automatically encrypted. Complete backups include account and audit records. Selected operating-system file providers, cloud folders, printers or other apps may receive copies under their own settings and policies. The operating system and location providers may also communicate over a network; offline record storage does not disable those services.

4. Optional device location

An explicit foreground capture action requests an operating-system location fix for a duty check-in. Saving the check-in is a separate action. The app does not implement background location tracking, and manual coordinates are available as an alternative. You can deny or revoke location permission in system settings.

A device permission or capture confirmation does not itself establish the agency’s legal basis for collecting a worker’s location. The agency must explain necessity, recipients and retention before use.

5. Security and retention

App sign-in and permissions restrict actions through the app. Live database and attachment files are not encrypted by the app; protect the device, its user accounts and storage. Encrypted backups require their passphrase, which the publisher cannot recover. Keep the passphrase securely and separately, and test restoration.

The agency must set retention and access rules, review exports and manage copies on every device and destination. Archiving a record is not erasure. There is no comprehensive automatic retention purge or all-records self-service deletion workflow. Deactivating a local account does not erase its activity or associated business records. Uninstalling on Windows must not be assumed to remove application data, and clearing app storage does not remove exported files, backups or copies elsewhere.

6. Rights and deletion requests

For access, correction, objection or deletion concerning agency records, contact the agency’s privacy contact first. NokNokware cannot remotely access or delete an agency’s offline records. The agency must verify authority, consider applicable legal duties and restrictions, and coordinate any appropriate action across its independent devices and backups.

For publisher-held support correspondence, contact benjaminthethird@gmail.com. We use information you send to respond, investigate problems and handle related privacy requests; we do not sell it or use it for advertising. Retention depends on the support purpose and applicable legal obligations. Requests are subject to proportionate identity verification and applicable exceptions; no universal deletion deadline is promised.

7. Support, third parties and updates

Send only a minimal description, app version and a synthetic example or carefully redacted screenshot. Do not send passwords, signing keys, full databases, complete backups, identity documents or actual GPS coordinates. If exceptional data access is necessary, agree on an authorized secure process first.

The app is a business workforce tool, rather than a service directed to children. Agency-entered incidents or visitor records may nevertheless concern children or other third parties; the agency must apply appropriate safeguards and notices.

This policy may change when the software or support practices change. Check the review date and the agency’s own notice. The related Account and data deletion requests page explains the available request routes; it is not an automatic deletion service.
