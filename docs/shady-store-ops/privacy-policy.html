Privacy Policy for Shady Store Ops

Last updated: October 1, 2026

NokNokware ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Shady Store Ops mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, how it is used, and how to contact us.

By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Information the App Handles

On this device

Sales, products, stock, categories, receipts, and staff accounts are stored in a database on the device. That data stays on the device unless online sync is turned on for the store.

  • Store name, address, phone, currency, tax, and receipt text you enter in Settings
  • Products, prices, stock counts, and categories
  • Sales, line items, and receipt history
  • Staff usernames, roles, and password hashes for cashiers and managers

Owner account

When you create a store, the App asks for an owner username, email address, and password. The password is stored as a hash on the device. The email and password are also kept so the App can register optional cloud access later.

Optional cloud sync

Online access is off until a super admin turns it on. When it is on, the App can send the store's sales data and account details to our Supabase project so the same shop can be used from more than one device. Until then, selling stays on the device and is not uploaded.

Advertising

The App shows banner ads through Google AdMob on phones and tablets. The register screen does not show ads. AdMob may use an advertising identifier and technical device data to serve and measure ads, under Google's own policies.

We do not sell your personal information or your sales data.

2. How We Use Information

  • Run the register, products, stock, sales history, and staff sign-in on the device
  • Remember the owner email and password so optional cloud sync can be turned on later
  • Sync store data to Supabase only when online access is on
  • Show ads through Google AdMob
  • Respond to support requests

3. How Information Is Shared

On-device sales data is not shared with other shops. If online sync is on, that store's data is stored in Supabase and is available to devices signed in to the same store.

Google AdMob receives the advertising data described above when ads are shown.

We may disclose information if required by law.

4. Retention and account deletion

On-device data remains until you delete the account, clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App. Cloud data, when the store is registered online, remains until the account is deleted.

The store owner can delete the account in the App under Settings → Delete account. That removes the owner login, staff logins, store profile, products, stock, sales, and settings on that device, and the online copy when one exists. Deletion in the App is immediate.

You can also request deletion without opening the App. The steps, including what is deleted, what is kept, and how long a request takes, are on the delete account page.

5. Your Choices

  • You can use the App offline. Cloud sync does not run while online access is off.
  • You can limit ad tracking in your device settings.
  • You can delete the account from Settings, or request deletion from the delete account page.
  • Clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it removes the local database. It does not by itself remove an online copy. Use Delete account or the web page for that.
