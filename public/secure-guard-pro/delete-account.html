Local accounts and agency records

SecureGuard Pro for Windows and Android stores independent records on each device. It does not provide a publisher-hosted account or a remote account deletion service. This page does not connect to your installation or automatically delete anything.

For a local app account, personnel record, duty check-in, visitor entry, incident, photo or other agency-held information, contact the agency or organization responsible for that installation. Ask its privacy contact or authorized administrator for access, correction or deletion assistance.

  1. Identify the agency and the kind of request. Give only the minimum information needed to locate the record through the agency’s approved channel.
  2. The agency should verify your identity and authority proportionately, explain any applicable retention or legal restrictions, and arrange permitted changes.
  3. Ask the agency to consider other devices, reports, source files, printouts, exports and backups. A change on one device does not update independent copies.

Deactivating a local account prevents sign-in; it does not erase business records or historical audit entries. Archiving records is not deletion. The current app has no comprehensive self-service account-and-all-records erasure workflow or automatic retention purge. Do not assume that a request will immediately remove every copy. An old backup can reintroduce records when restored.

NokNokware cannot remotely access or delete an agency’s offline records. Clearing Android app storage can remove app-held local data, but exported files and copies elsewhere remain. Uninstalling the Windows app must not be assumed to remove its data. Do not clear storage or replace records without the agency’s authorization and a recovery plan.

Information held by the publisher

This also includes licensing identifiers, installation public keys, platform, activation/expiry times and status held by NokNokware Licensing on Appwrite Cloud. Identify the product and license ID if available, without sending the activation key. A licensing metadata deletion request may affect entitlement availability; it does not remotely erase agency records. Applicable retention obligations and proportionate verification still apply.

For support correspondence or other information you supplied directly to NokNokware, email benjaminthethird@gmail.com with the subject “SecureGuard Pro privacy request”. State whether you seek access, correction or deletion and which support exchange it concerns. Use the email address involved where possible.

We may need proportionate verification or clarification before acting. We will explain the scope and outcome, including any information that must be retained for applicable legal obligations or legitimate handling of the request. There is no fixed universal deletion period promised on this page. This route concerns publisher-held information; it cannot erase agency-controlled records on your device.

Keep requests minimal

Do not email passwords, signing keys, full databases, complete backups, identity documents or actual GPS coordinates. Use synthetic examples and redacted screenshots for technical questions. If exceptional information sharing is necessary, arrange an authorized secure process first. Do not send another person’s records to the publisher merely to ask for deletion.

For the full software handling description, see the SecureGuard Pro privacy policy. The agency’s own privacy notice must provide its responsible contact and applicable rights process.
