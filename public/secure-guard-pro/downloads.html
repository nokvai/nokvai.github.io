A license is required for editing

Downloading the app does not include a license. Activate a unique license inside the app with an internet connection. Each Windows installation or Android installation needs its own license, valid for six calendar months from activation.

After activation, licensed editing works offline until the signed expiry, subject to local license validation. Agency records stay on the device. Read the licensing details for renewal, expiry and device-transfer information.

Before upgrading an existing installation

Create an encrypted backup in the existing app and keep its passphrase securely. Keep your original app and data until you have verified the upgrade. Contact support before reinstalling or clearing app storage, since activation belongs to that installation.

An Android debug build and a production release may use different signing certificates, which can prevent an in-place upgrade. If Android reports a signature conflict, stop and contact support with the app version. Do not remove an installation containing records to resolve the conflict.
