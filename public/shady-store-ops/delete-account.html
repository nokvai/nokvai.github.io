Delete your Shady Store Ops account

NokNokware · Shady Store Ops · Last updated: October 1, 2026

This page is for the store owner who created a Shady Store Ops account. You can request deletion here without installing or opening the app, or you can delete the account inside the app.

Delete inside the app

This finishes immediately on this device, and deletes the online copy when the store was registered and the device is online.

  1. Open Shady Store Ops and sign in as the store owner.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Choose Delete account.
  4. Type the store name and confirm.

Request deletion without the app

You do not need to sign in. Send an email and we will delete the account.

  1. Email benjaminthethird@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line “Delete my Shady Store Ops account”.
  3. Include the store name and the owner email address used in the app.

We complete email requests within 30 days. We email you when the account has been deleted. The request email itself is deleted after the account is removed.

What is deleted

  • The owner username, email, and password
  • Staff usernames, roles, and password hashes
  • Store name, address, phone, tax, and receipt text
  • Products, categories, stock, sales, and receipt copies stored by the app
  • The online store and its rows in our Supabase project, when one exists

What is kept

  • No store or account data is kept after deletion finishes.
  • Advertising data already sent to Google AdMob is held by Google, not in the Shady Store Ops account. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Another device that used the same store can still hold a local copy until you delete the account there, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app. The online copy is removed by either deletion path above.
