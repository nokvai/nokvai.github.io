Metal Profiler Child Safety Standards

Publisher: NokNokware · App: Metal Profiler · Package: com.noknokware.metalprofiler
Last updated: 27 August 2026

Metal Profiler is a social community app for adult metalheads, musicians, and fans. These standards describe how we prohibit, prevent, detect, and respond to child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

1. Age and audience

  • Metal Profiler is not directed at children under 13.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
  • Users must meet the minimum age required by applicable law in their country to create an account and use social features.
  • If we learn that an account belongs to a child under 13, or is being used to exploit a minor, we will take appropriate action, including account removal and content deletion.

2. Prohibited content and conduct

The following are strictly forbidden on Metal Profiler:

  • Any sexual content involving minors (anyone under 18), including CSAM and grooming
  • Sharing, soliciting, or linking to CSAM or other child exploitation material
  • Sexualization of minors in text, images, profiles, posts, messages, or links
  • Attempting to contact minors for sexual purposes
  • Any other activity that endangers children or facilitates CSAE

Violations result in content removal, account suspension or permanent ban, and reporting to relevant authorities when required or appropriate.

3. Prevention and moderation

  • User-generated content (profiles, posts, messages, media links, and uploads) is subject to review when reported or otherwise flagged.
  • Authorized reviewers / Super Admin may remove content, reject verification, suspend accounts, and preserve records needed for safety investigations.
  • We may limit features, block accounts, or delete material that violates these standards without prior notice.

4. How to report child safety concerns

Anyone (users, parents/guardians, or third parties) can report suspected CSAE or child safety concerns related to Metal Profiler by emailing:

Child safety contact: bmeliseo@gmail.com

Please include, when available:

  1. Username(s) or profile links involved
  2. Description of the concern and when it occurred
  3. Screenshots, message IDs, post links, or other evidence
  4. Your contact email so we can follow up if needed

Do not attach or forward CSAM itself in email. Describe the location of the material in the app (username, post, conversation) so we can locate and remove it through our systems.

5. Response process

  1. We review child safety reports on a priority basis.
  2. We remove or restrict violating content and accounts as appropriate.
  3. We preserve relevant records as required for legal, security, or law-enforcement purposes.
  4. Where required or appropriate, we report to regional or national authorities (including organizations such as NCMEC CyberTipline for U.S.-relevant CSAM reports) and cooperate with lawful requests.

6. Compliance commitment

NokNokware is committed to complying with applicable child safety laws and platform policies, including Google Play requirements for standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation. These standards may be updated as our product, laws, or reporting channels evolve. The current version will remain published at this URL.
